Photo & Signature

A utility app that helps you crop, resize, compress, validate, create and prepare photos and signatures for job applications, admission forms, passport/ID-related use and similar purposes.

com.picsig.najafi Effective: 7 October 2026

Quick Summary

  • 📱 Photos & signatures are processed locally on your device.
  • 🔒 No account or sign-in is required.
  • 🚫 Your photos and signatures are never sold.
  • 📤 Files leave your device only when you choose to share them.
01

Photos and Signatures

  • Photos selected through Android's system Photo Picker, photos taken by the user, signatures drawn inside the app and uploaded signature images are processed locally on the user's device.
  • The developer does not intentionally upload users' photos, signatures or edited output files to a developer-operated server.
  • Finished photos and signatures may be saved to the user's device. Recent-file references and app settings may also be stored locally on the device.
02

Sharing

  • The app allows users to share finished photos or signatures using Android's system sharing feature. A file is transferred to another application only when the user explicitly chooses to share it.
  • The receiving application or service may process that file according to its own privacy policy.
03

Advertising

  • Photo & Signature uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements.
  • Google Mobile Ads may automatically process information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic/performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google states these may be used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention/security purposes and are encrypted in transit.
  • The app also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Google recommends checking consent status when the app launches and presenting privacy options where required.
04

Internet and Remote Configuration

  • Internet access is used for advertisements, consent services and remote configuration.
  • The app may download a small HTTPS configuration file from GitHub to remotely enable or disable advertisements. This remote configuration does not intentionally contain users' photos, signatures or other personal content.
05

Data Retention

  • Photos and signatures processed by the app remain on the user's device unless the user saves or shares them.
  • Users can delete locally saved files, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Data processed by Google AdMob or other third-party services is governed by those providers' respective retention and privacy practices.
06

Account and Personal Information

  • Photo & Signature does not require users to create an account or sign in for its core features.
  • The developer does not intentionally collect users' names, passwords, phone numbers or email addresses through the photo/signature editing features.
07

Children's Privacy

Photo & Signature is a general-purpose utility app and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

08

Sale of Data

The developer does not sell users' photos, signatures or personal content.

09

Security
