Privacy Policy

Bangla Audio Book turns documents, pasted text and public web pages into spoken audio. File import, editing and bundled Bengali narration work offline; public webpage links and advertising require internet. This policy explains how the app and its service providers handle information.

1. Who is responsible?

This policy applies to Bangla Audio Book (also described as Bangla Audio Book), provided by Rashed Hossain Najafi (“we”, “us” or “the developer”). The app does not require a developer-operated account. Its core reading library is primarily stored on your device, but it is not accurate to say the entire app collects no data: advertising and analytics SDKs communicate with their providers.

2. Your documents, text and reading records

  • When you select a PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT or supported image, the app reads it using Android's document picker and extracts readable text on your device. This feature does not upload the document to a developer-operated document server.
  • When you paste text, the app processes that text locally. Saved book titles, extracted text, bookmarks, notes (where available), progress, reading settings and reading-credit transactions are kept in on-device app storage.
  • Bengali narration uses one bundled, on-device Bengali neural voice model with one fixed deep male-sounding speaker. Audio is synthesized on the phone, not sent to a speech server. The app does not offer premium voice choices or silently substitute robotic device voices for Bengali text. Other supported languages can use an installed Android TTS engine.
  • You choose which files to import. The app does not request unrestricted access to all device files just to import a document.
  • Credits in this app are internal reading allowances only. They are not cash, cryptocurrency or transferable balances and cannot be redeemed for money.

3. Internet connections and external services

Offline reading. Local file import, pasted text, saved books, editing, credits and bundled Bengali neural narration work without internet. A network connection is required to fetch website links, load rewarded ads, check updates and communicate with optional advertising and analytics services. An interrupted connection does not stop an offline Bengali reading.

Web page reading. If you provide a public HTTPS address, the app contacts that web server to retrieve its HTML and extract readable text. If the initial response lacks article content, an isolated Android WebView may load the page with JavaScript to extract text. The site and any third-party resources it loads may observe normal requests, IP address and technical information. Website privacy practices apply. The app does not sign in for you or upload imported local documents to that site.

On-device natural Bengali speech. After you review/skip text and explicitly approve playback, a bundled Bengali neural model synthesizes only the included passage on the device, with storytelling pauses and a fixed male-sounding speaker. No text is transmitted to Microsoft Azure or a publisher narration proxy. Generated audio is temporarily cached in app-private storage and deleted after the session. If local synthesis fails, reading pauses without silently switching to robotic Android TTS.

Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app includes banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob and advertising technology providers may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising or app identifiers, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), device characteristics and diagnostics for displaying and measuring ads, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Applicable consent forms or advertising privacy controls are shown when required. Not consenting to personalized ads does not itself block content processing when connected; ad availability may change.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics; GitHub ad controls. Firebase may process usage events, app/device identifiers, crash details and technical diagnostics. Public GitHub-hosted JSON switches independently control banner, interstitial and rewarded ad availability; no book text is sent to this settings URL. We record action event names, not complete imported books. Exception messages may incidentally contain limited URL or filename context.

Google Play services. Checking for an app update or opening the Play Store can involve Google Play and related services under their terms and privacy notices.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy information · Google advertising technologies.

Bundled voice software and attributions: Piper bn_BD-google-medium model (MIT repository marking; see model card), sherpa-onnx (Apache 2.0) and eSpeak NG data (GPL-related notices). Full sources and redistribution conditions must be checked before publishing.

5. Permissions and background audio

  • Internet and network state: checking connectivity for the online website, ads, analytics and update features; offline books and Bengali narration do not need internet.
  • Notifications: optional system notifications for audio playback and long-running processing; the notification permission is requested where Android requires it.
  • Foreground media playback: keeping user-started audio playing when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
  • Wake lock: assisting uninterrupted speech while audio is active.
  • Document picker: scoped access to files you explicitly select, without blanket library access.

Android and your device's battery policy may still stop background work or playback. You can change notification permission in Android Settings.

6. Your privacy choices

  • Files: Import only material you want processed. Open Library to delete individual books or several selected books along with their saved reading progress.
  • Ads: Open More → Privacy & Diagnostics → Ad privacy choices where the Google UMP privacy options form is available for your region. Rewarded advertisements are voluntary.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: This version does not provide a separate in-app Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics opt-out. You can stop using the app or uninstall it; for data-related questions, contact the developer below. Privacy controls in Android and Google accounts may also apply.
  • Text-to-speech: You can review, delete or skip text before agreeing to narration. Only included Bengali passages are synthesized locally; no narration passage is sent to an external voice provider. No robotic Bengali device fallback or premium voice list is used. Other supported languages may still rely on installed Android speech data.
  • Device data: Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally erases on-device app records. This action cannot necessarily erase information already processed by third-party providers; their retention and deletion procedures apply.

7. Storage, security and deletion

Locally saved books, reading information and the internal credit ledger remain on the device until you remove them or clear the app's storage. The app disables Android's regular app-data backup for this package. Imported web content is saved locally only when you choose a saving option. We use Android's app sandbox for local records and HTTPS for the public web pages the app supports.

Data collected by Google AdMob, Firebase and other independent service providers is subject to those providers' security, retention and deletion practices. The developer cannot promise that uninstalling immediately removes all provider-held diagnostic or advertising records. For privacy requests, use the verified contact below and describe the app and your request; do not email private book contents or sensitive device identifiers. We will review requests in light of what data we actually control and applicable legal obligations.

No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android and your speech engine up to date.

8. Children and audience

This general-purpose reading utility is intended for people aged 13 and older; it is not designed for children under 13. Teen users may be treated as children under local laws. We must apply the applicable age-related ad, consent and privacy protections. Users choose their own reading material, including external websites, which the developer does not curate or endorse.
