Jafari Events Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Developer: R.H Najafi
Android package: com.calendar.najafi
Privacy summary: Jafari Events is an offline-first calendar application. Core calendar functions, Hijri calculations, the bundled Islamic event database, local moon-sighting adjustments, reminder settings, and other core preferences work on the device without requiring an account. The developer does not operate a server that stores your calendar selections, Hijri adjustments, moon-sighting settings, or religious-use data. The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and Google Play services to check for and deliver app updates.

1. Information stored on your device

Jafari Events stores settings required to provide its features locally on your device. These may include:

  • Hijri date adjustment settings;
  • locally confirmed Hijri month starts;
  • 29-day or 30-day month-length preferences;
  • notification preferences;
  • sound and vibration preferences;
  • privacy acknowledgement status; and
  • other application settings required for normal operation.

These settings are stored in the app's private local storage and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. They normally remain on the device until you change them, clear/reset app data, or uninstall the application.

2. Information Jafari Events does not directly request

Jafari Events does not require registration or a user account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, phone number, postal address, contacts, precise GPS location, photos, videos, microphone recordings, camera content, SMS messages, call logs, or broad file/storage access.

The current version of the app does not request Android location permission.

3. Firebase Analytics

Jafari Events uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, including active/realtime usage, app launches, sessions, screen activity, and general interaction patterns. This helps the developer improve reliability, usability, and future features.

Depending on Google's SDK behavior, device settings, and service configuration, Firebase Analytics may automatically process limited technical and usage data such as:

  • app launches, sessions, screen views, and app interaction events;
  • app version and installation-related information;
  • device type, operating system version, language, and similar device/app information;
  • Firebase app-instance or similar analytics identifiers;
  • general diagnostics and performance-related information; and
  • coarse geographic information such as country or region inferred by Google from technical data such as an IP address.

Jafari Events does not send your locally stored Hijri adjustments, moon-sighting settings, calendar selections, or religious event preferences to Firebase Analytics.

The app does not use Google Mobile Ads and does not request the Android Advertising ID permission for analytics. Advertising-ID and AdServices-ID permissions are explicitly excluded from the current app configuration.

4. Google Play update checks and in-app updates

When an internet connection is available, Jafari Events may use Google Play services to check whether a newer version of the application is available.

If an update is available, the app may display an update message and start the official Google Play in-app update flow or open the app's Google Play listing.

This update-checking feature does not send your calendar selections, Hijri adjustments, moon-sighting choices, locally stored event preferences, or religious-use information to the developer.

5. Notifications, sound, vibration, and alarm scheduling

If event reminders are enabled, Jafari Events schedules local notifications on your device.

Android notification, sound, vibration, wake-lock, exact-alarm, reboot, time-change, and timezone-change capabilities may be used to deliver or restore reminders. Notification preferences are stored locally on your device.

The app also provides a Test Notification feature so that users can verify notification sound and vibration behavior on their device.

6. Offline functionality

The core calendar is designed to work without an internet connection. Hijri calculations and the primary Islamic event database are bundled with or stored locally by the application.

If optional online services such as Firebase Analytics or Google Play update checking are unavailable, the core calendar, local event database, date adjustment, and reminders are designed to remain usable.

7. Advertising

The current version of Jafari Events does not display third-party advertisements and does not include Google AdMob or the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

8. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your locally stored personal information, calendar settings, Hijri adjustments, moon-sighting settings, or religious-use information.

Jafari Events does not upload these locally stored calendar preferences to a developer-operated server.

Firebase Analytics and Google Play services may process technical or usage information as independent Google services under Google's own privacy policies, service terms, and applicable law.

9. Data retention and deletion

Settings stored locally by Jafari Events remain on the device until they are changed, reset, cleared through the app or Android system settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

The app provides options to reset settings and clear app data. Android system settings may also be used to clear the application's stored data.

Information processed by Firebase Analytics or other Google services is subject to Google's own data-retention and deletion practices.

10. Data security

Jafari Events is designed to minimize the amount of app-specific information transmitted online.

Data transmitted to Firebase Analytics or Google Play services is protected in transit using secure transport such as HTTPS/TLS as provided by Google.

No electronic transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable technical measures are used by the app and its service providers to protect information.

11. Children's privacy

Jafari Events does not provide user accounts or forms for submitting personal information directly to the developer.

Parents or guardians who have questions regarding privacy may contact the developer using the contact information below.

12. Third-party services

Jafari Events may use or interact with third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase Analytics; and
  • Google Play services / Google Play in-app updates.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users who want detailed information about how Google processes information should review Google's applicable privacy and Firebase documentation.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, permissions, SDKs, analytics configuration, third-party services, or legal obligations change.

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
