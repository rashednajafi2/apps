Jafari Events Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Developer: R.H Najafi
Android package: com.calendar.najafi
Privacy summary: Jafari Events is an offline-first app. The core calendar, Hijri calculations, bundled religious-event database, local moon-sighting adjustments, startup tips, theme settings, and reminder preferences work on the device without requiring an account or an internet connection. The app itself does not operate a server that stores your calendar settings or religious-use data.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Jafari Events stores only the settings needed to provide its features, such as Hijri date adjustment, locally confirmed month starts or month lengths, notification settings, vibration and sound preferences, startup-tip preference, dark/light theme preference, and related app settings. These settings remain in the app's private local storage unless you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

2. Information Jafari Events does not request

The app does not require account registration and does not ask for your name, phone number, address, contacts, precise location, photos, videos, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, or broad file/storage access. The current app does not request Android location permissions.

3. Advertising and Google User Messaging Platform

Jafari Events uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent choices where required. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for purposes that include advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may also be used by Google to estimate a device's general location.

The app requests advertisements only when the Google consent state allows ad requests. Jafari Events also uses a conservative maximum ad-content rating and disables publisher privacy personalization in its Mobile Ads request configuration. Where Google makes additional privacy options available for a user or region, those options can be opened from the app's Privacy section.

4. Remote feature switches

When an internet connection is available, the app may download a small HTTPS JSON configuration file from a public GitHub-hosted endpoint. This file contains simple on/off switches for optional features such as advertisements, startup tips, and update checking. The request does not upload your calendar database, selected dates, Hijri adjustments, moon-sighting choices, reminder preferences, or religious/event content. As with ordinary internet requests, the hosting provider may receive standard connection data such as an IP address and technical request information under its own privacy practices.

5. Google Play update checks

If update checking is enabled and the device is online, the app may use Google Play's in-app update service to determine whether a newer version is available. The app does not send your calendar content or local religious-use data as part of this feature.

6. Notifications, sound, vibration, and reboot handling

If reminders are enabled, Jafari Events schedules local notifications for calendar events. Android notification, vibration, wake-lock, and reboot-related permissions are used only to deliver or restore these local reminders. Notification preferences remain stored locally on your device.

7. Offline functionality

The core calendar is designed to remain usable without internet access. The bundled event database and Hijri calendar logic are stored with the app. If optional online services such as ads, remote feature switches, or update checks are unavailable, the core calendar should continue to function.

8. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your personal information. Jafari Events does not upload your locally stored calendar preferences to the developer. Third-party services used by the app, particularly Google AdMob/UMP and Google Play services, may process data independently as described in their own privacy documentation and according to the consent and privacy choices available to you.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local settings remain on your device until they are changed, cleared from the app's Privacy section or Android system settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. Data processed independently by Google, GitHub, or other service providers is subject to those providers' own retention and deletion practices.

10. Security

Jafari Events blocks cleartext network traffic for its own remote configuration and uses HTTPS for that request. Google Mobile Ads data is transmitted using secure transport as documented by Google. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but the app is designed to minimize the amount of app-specific information sent online.

11. Children's privacy

Jafari Events does not require an account and does not intentionally ask users, including children, to submit personal information directly to the developer through the app. Advertising and consent behavior is handled through Google services and should be configured consistently with the app's selected Google Play target audience and any applicable child-directed requirements.

12. Third-party services

The app may interact with services operated by third parties, including Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play, and GitHub. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. You should review those policies if you want more information about their processing practices.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, permissions, SDKs, advertising configuration, service providers, or legal obligations change. When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
