This Privacy Policy explains how data may be accessed, collected, processed, transmitted, stored, or shared by the app, its integrated SDKs, and third-party services.

3. Location

The app may request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and/or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. These permissions are used in the foreground while the app is in use for prayer times, Qibla, and location-aware Hijri/calendar functionality.

The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and does not use geofencing. Before the first Android location permission request, the app presents an in-app disclosure explaining the location use.

For prayer-time and calendar requests, latitude and longitude may be sent over HTTPS to the AlAdhan API. Qibla bearing is primarily calculated on the device. Android/device geocoding services may also process coordinates to resolve a human-readable location name.

If location permission is denied, non-location features remain available, while location-dependent results may be limited or may use previously cached information where available.

4. Google AdMob, Advertising, and Advertising ID

The app uses Google AdMob banner advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain data for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. This may include IP address, app/user product interactions, diagnostic information, Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device/account identifiers where applicable.

The Android Advertising ID can be reset or deleted using device settings. Firebase Analytics Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled in the app configuration; however, Google AdMob may separately process advertising-related identifiers and data as part of the Mobile Ads service.

Where required by applicable law or Google policy, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may display consent or privacy messages. An Ad Privacy Choices entry may appear in Settings when required.

5. Firebase Analytics

Google Firebase Analytics is used for active-user, realtime, session, screen, engagement, and technical usage reporting. Analytics collection remains disabled until the required first-run analytics disclosure is affirmatively acknowledged.

After acknowledgement, Analytics remains enabled as part of the app's required analytics setup and there is no Analytics on/off toggle in the app Settings.

Firebase Analytics may process an app-instance identifier, app lifecycle/session/screen interactions, app version, device/OS technical information, and coarse geographic information derived by Google. The app does not intentionally send a user's name, email address, phone number, search text, custom Analytics user ID, or precise GPS coordinates to Firebase Analytics.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics is optional. If the user enables it, Crashlytics may process crash reports, non-fatal errors, ANRs, stack traces, relevant application state, installation/session diagnostics, and device metadata. Users can enable or disable Crashlytics from the app Settings.

7. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used for remote push notifications. When notifications are available and enabled, Firebase may create or process an installation/registration token and necessary technical metadata so a notification can be delivered to a specific app installation.

When notification access is unavailable, the app is configured to avoid unnecessary FCM auto-initialization. Some event and Adhan notifications are scheduled locally on the device.

8. Today's Verse

Today's Verse selects a short Qur'an verse and its English meaning from the bundled local database. Religious database content is not uploaded to a server for this feature.

To avoid immediate repetition and enforce the display interval, the app may store the last-shown time and previous tip identifier locally on the device. The feature is designed to appear no more than once every 12 hours, which means at most twice within a 24-hour period.

Users can enable or disable Today's Verse in Settings. The developer may also enable or disable the feature through a remote feature flag.

9. GitHub Remote Configuration

The app may download a public JSON configuration file hosted on GitHub to control whether advertising and certain features are enabled. As part of this configuration request, the app does not intentionally upload a user's name, phone number, email address, precise location, or religious content to GitHub.

The remote configuration is used in the current app-process memory and may be refreshed periodically in the background while the app process is running. GitHub may process standard network metadata, including IP address, under its own privacy practices.

10. Online Audio and External Web Services

When a user chooses online Qur'an, Dua, or Ziyarat audio, the app may send HTTPS requests to external audio hosting providers such as mp3.duas.org. Those providers may receive ordinary network metadata. The app may also open the developer website or Google Play pages when the user selects those actions.

11. Copy and Share

Copy and Share features for Arabic text, meanings, transliterations, Qur'an content, duas, ziyarat, sahifa, and other content operate only after direct user action.

Copy places selected text on the Android clipboard. Share opens the Android share sheet and sends the content to the app or service selected by the user. The app does not control the privacy practices of the selected destination app or service.

12. Local and Offline Data

The bundled religious database, bookmarks, favourites, continue-reading position, fonts, Adhan/vibration preferences, Hijri adjustment, Today's Verse timing, cached prayer/location data, and other settings are primarily stored locally on the device.

Android backup/device transfer is disabled for the app's private app data.

13. Security

The app uses HTTPS/TLS for the identified external services and is configured to block cleartext HTTP traffic. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. The app is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

14. Retention and Deletion

The app does not operate user accounts or an identity-linked central user-profile database. Local app data can be removed by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Data processed by Google/Firebase, Google AdMob, GitHub, AlAdhan, online-audio providers, or other third-party services is subject to the applicable provider's retention and deletion practices. Removing local app data does not automatically guarantee deletion of historical records already processed by a third-party provider.

15. Data Sale and Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent user information. Data may nevertheless be processed or transferred to Google/Firebase/AdMob, AlAdhan, GitHub, and online-audio providers for app functionality, analytics, advertising, notification delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention as described in this policy.

Google Play's formal definition of “sharing” may differ from ordinary-language use of that word. The Google Play Data Safety declaration should be maintained according to current app behavior and Google Play definitions.
