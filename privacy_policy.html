1. Who is responsible?

This policy applies to AI Audiobook: Text to Speech (also described as AI Audiobook & Text Reader), provided by Rashed Hossain Najafi (“we”, “us” or “the developer”). The app does not require a developer-operated account. Its core reading library is primarily stored on your device, but it is not accurate to say the entire app collects no data: advertising and analytics SDKs communicate with their providers.

2. Your documents, text and reading records

  • When you select a PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT or supported image, the app reads it using Android's document picker and extracts readable text on your device. This feature does not upload the document to a developer-operated document server.
  • When you paste text, the app processes that text locally. Saved book titles, extracted text, bookmarks, notes (where available), progress, reading settings and reading-credit transactions are kept in on-device app storage.
  • The app uses local voice and language-processing components where available. A separate text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed or chosen on your phone may process narration differently, including over the internet depending on its provider and installed voices. Consult that engine's own privacy policy and install an offline voice for offline narration.
  • You choose which files to import. The app does not request unrestricted access to all device files just to import a document.
  • Credits in this app are internal reading allowances only. They are not cash, cryptocurrency or transferable balances and cannot be redeemed for money.

3. Internet connections and external services

Web page reading. If you provide a public HTTPS address, the app contacts that web server to retrieve its HTML and extract readable text. That site's operator and infrastructure can observe normal request information, such as your IP address, requested page, network timing and request headers. The website's own privacy practices apply. The app does not sign in to websites for you or send an imported local document to that website.

Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app includes banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob and advertising technology providers may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising or app identifiers, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), device characteristics and diagnostics for displaying and measuring ads, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Applicable consent forms or advertising privacy controls are shown when required. Not consenting to personalized ads does not itself stop your local reading functions; ad availability may change.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These services collect usage-event information, app/device identifiers, app and device properties, crash details, stack traces and technical diagnostics so the developer can understand feature usage and fix stability problems. We record event names for app actions, not the complete text of imported books. Crash reports or exception messages might incidentally reveal limited technical context, including a URL or filename contained in an exception, so avoid sensitive names where possible.

Google Play services. Checking for an app update or opening the Play Store can involve Google Play and related services under their terms and privacy notices.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy information · Google advertising technologies.

5. Permissions and background audio

  • Internet and network state: loading requested web pages, ads, analytics and update checks.
  • Notifications: optional system notifications for audio playback and long-running processing; the notification permission is requested where Android requires it.
  • Foreground media playback: keeping user-started audio playing when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
  • Wake lock: assisting uninterrupted speech while audio is active.
  • Document picker: scoped access to files you explicitly select, without blanket library access.

Android and your device's battery policy may still stop background work or playback. You can change notification permission in Android Settings.

6. Your privacy choices

  • Files: Import only material you want processed. Open Library to delete individual books or several selected books along with their saved reading progress.
  • Ads: Open More → Privacy & Diagnostics → Ad privacy choices where the Google UMP privacy options form is available for your region. Rewarded advertisements are voluntary.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: This version does not provide a separate in-app Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics opt-out. You can stop using the app or uninstall it; for data-related questions, contact the developer below. Privacy controls in Android and Google accounts may also apply.
  • Text-to-speech: Choose an offline-installed TTS engine/voice in the Android system's language and speech settings if you want to minimize network speech processing.
  • Device data: Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally erases on-device app records. This action cannot necessarily erase information already processed by third-party providers; their retention and deletion procedures apply.

7. Storage, security and deletion

Locally saved books, reading information and the internal credit ledger remain on the device until you remove them or clear the app's storage. The app disables Android's regular app-data backup for this package. Imported web content is saved locally only when you choose a saving option. We use Android's app sandbox for local records and HTTPS for the public web pages the app supports.

Data collected by Google AdMob, Firebase and other independent service providers is subject to those providers' security, retention and deletion practices. The developer cannot promise that uninstalling immediately removes all provider-held diagnostic or advertising records. For privacy requests, use the verified contact below and describe the app and your request; do not email private book contents or sensitive device identifiers. We will review requests in light of what data we actually control and applicable legal obligations.

No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android and your speech engine up to date.

8. Children and audience

This is a general-purpose text-to-speech reading utility, not an app designed specifically for young children. The developer must confirm the actual intended age groups and configure Google Play's target-audience settings, advertising and any legally required parental protections accordingly. Users may import their own reading material; the developer does not curate or endorse external website content.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, advertising partners, analytics configuration or legal requirements change. The current version will appear at the publicly hosted Privacy Policy page and in updated app releases as appropriate. Material changes will be disclosed when required.
